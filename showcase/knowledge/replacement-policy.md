# Volt & Co synthetic replacement policy

Reference: VOLT-REPLACEMENT-2026-11
Effective date and demonstration clock: 2026-11-11
Scope: fictional Volt & Co products and synthetic demonstration orders only.

An identified product batch with an active recall is eligible for a human
replacement assessment even after the standard return window. Confirm both the
order and batch using the business tools. A recall result is not proof that the
customer owns a particular order.

Standard unopened returns use a 30-day window from delivery. Warranty assessment
is a separate human support process, not an automatic refund or replacement.
For unknown orders, missing batches, or batches absent from the register, ask for
the missing evidence or escalate. Never equate missing data with no recall.

The assistant can explain eligibility and recommend the next step. It cannot
approve a replacement, ship goods, issue refunds, or change records. The tools
in this showcase are read-only.

Reports of a swollen, very hot, smoking, or burnt device must be escalated to a
human support representative. The assistant must not offer repair workarounds.
